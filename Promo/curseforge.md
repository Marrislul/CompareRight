**Compare Right** keeps item comparison in the same order every time: the item you hover starts at your mouse, and your equipped item sits to the right of it.

The game puts the "Equipped" tooltip on whichever side has more room, so on the right half of the screen it suddenly shows up on the left. That kept throwing me off, so I made this.

It works wherever the game compares items: bags, vendors, quest rewards and tooltips from other addons. There's nothing to set up.
